# CareerBot AI 🤖🎓

CareerBot AI is an intelligent career guidance chatbot designed to assist students, fresh graduates, and professionals in making informed career decisions. Through an interactive and user-friendly chat interface, the chatbot provides instant responses to career-related queries and offers practical guidance to support users throughout their professional journey. From exploring career paths to improving job readiness, CareerBot AI aims to simplify career planning and make reliable guidance accessible anytime and anywhere.

## Live Demo

🌐 https://dhakshanals.github.io/CodeAlpha_career-chatbox-Ai/

## Features

* 🎯 Career Path Recommendations
* 📄 Resume Writing Tips and Suggestions
* 🎤 Interview Preparation Guidance
* 💼 Job Search Assistance
* 💰 Salary Insights for Various Career Roles
* 📚 Skills and Course Recommendations
* ⚡ Instant Chat-Based Responses
* 📱 Responsive and User-Friendly Interface
